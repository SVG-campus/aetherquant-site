Frequently Asked Questions

What is Convergent Cross Mapping (CCM)?

CCM is a mathematical technique used to identify non-linear causal relationships between two dynamical systems. Unlike linear correlation, CCM reconstructs shadow attractors from time-lagged variables to determine if states in one manifold can reliably predict states in another.

How does Topological Data Analysis (TDA) aid asset allocation?

TDA uses algebraic topology to extract structural features from cloud data. In volatile regimes, TDA identifies transient attractor voids that standard statistical indicators miss, warning quant engines to scale down exposure before systemic breakdowns.

Why use GARCH estimation over raw volatility?

GARCH modeling accounts for volatility clustering—the empirical fact that high-volatility days tend to follow high-volatility days. This dynamic modeling allows for proactive leverage scaling, keeping drawdowns within predetermined parameters.
